# DEV(毁灭篇)故事线中涉及的 DEV 卡表外角色——原名与上下文(供翻译)

**用途**:此文件收集 DEV 故事语境中提到的、不在 DEV 卡表内的角色,附英文原文上下文,可整体交翻译。
**来源**:TVTropes Characters/BattleCon + 奶牛关 BCO 译文(2026-11-26 拉取)。官方 DEV 故事册(Preview-Guide-Lores.pdf)未能下载,此表以二级来源为准,与官方册冲突时以官方为准[待核]。
**计数**:共 16 名(按故事出场序)。

---

## 1. Solar Saint Avenlia(耀阳圣徒阿维利亚)——四英雄之一
> "The ancestor in question is Solar Saint Avenlia, one of the Four Heroes who fought in the Centennial wars, two hundred years ago." (Magdelina 段落)
> "Famous Ancestor: Descended from Avelina, one of the four heroes who struck down Rexan." (Eligor 段落)
> 奶牛关(吕克段落): "吕克与其他三位英雄,分别为瑞利卡尔的瑟维、杰弗里的阿维利亚和沙海利姆的戴尔卡多,合作并踏上了阻止瑞克森的路途。"

## 2. Cervey of Relicar(瑞利卡尔的瑟维)——四英雄之一
> 奶牛关(吕克段落): "吕克与其他三位英雄,分别为瑞利卡尔的瑟维、杰弗里的阿维利亚和沙海利姆的戴尔卡多,合作并踏上了阻止瑞克森的路途。"
> [待核] 英文名仅见社区转写"Cervey",待官方故事册核。

## 3. Delcardo of Sanghalim(沙海利姆的戴尔卡多)——四英雄之一
> 同上吕克段落。
> [待核] 英文名仅见社区转写"Delcardo",待官方故事册核。

## 4. Dragon King Greyheart(龙族之王格雷哈特/灰心)——阿德婕娜之夫
> "Among them, the Dragon King Greyheart was the most powerful, standing as an almost godly ruler among his peers." (Adjenna 段落)
> "The Dragon King Greyheart was too powerful to truly die, and so he was imprisoned in the deep well below the grand colossus, Helim Mekhit."
> 关联地名: Helim Mekhit(巨像赫利姆·梅基特)

## 5. Marmelee(玛梅丽)——龙族公主/龙王之女/阿德婕娜之女
> "One Bad Mother: Isn't the greatest mother to her daughter Marmalee." (Adjenna 段落)
> 奶牛关(卡琳段落): "他们被一同送给龙族公主玛梅丽以成为她的贴身护卫后,卡琳和雅格加入了玛梅丽阻止她的父亲龙王涅槃的密谋。"
> [待核] 玛梅丽在故事中有阻止其父"龙王涅槃"的密谋——此"龙王"是否即 Greyheart,待官方故事册核。

## 6. Aaron Ross(亚伦·罗斯博士)——威劳迪科学家
> "She was created by Doctor Aaron Ross, an infamous Willati scientist, whose work was getting dangerously close to banned research." (Kehrolyn 段落)
> "Her natural aptitude for this branch of science landed her a position as assistant researcher for the famed alchemist Aaron Ross." (Lixis 段落)

## 7. August(奥古斯特)——原型变形者/卡罗琳之"姐"
> "After the Magisterial Council found out that his work had crossed the line, and Kehrolyn's 'sister' August had shown her capabilities by destroying a military battalion, Dr. Ross' laboratory and research were destroyed" (Kehrolyn 段落)
> "While working in this role, Lixis aided in the creation of August, the homunculus that sparked the Magisterial Council to deem Ross' research dangerous enough to destroy." (Lixis 段落)
> "Kehrolyn is not as powerful as her 'sister' August, but this is because she has built-in limitations on her transformations."

## 8. Bors Vilnar(鲍斯·维尔纳)——威劳迪魔导士
